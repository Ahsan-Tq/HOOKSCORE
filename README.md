# HookScore

Most creators guess when it comes to hooks. They write something that "feels right" and hope it performs.
HookScore removes the guesswork. It analyzes your short-form video hook and gives it a score out of 100 — based on structure, not instinct.

---

## The Problem

The first 3 seconds determine everything. If your hook does not stop the scroll, nothing else matters — not your edit, not your CTA, not your content. Yet most creators have no structured way to evaluate a hook before publishing. They rely on gut feeling, and gut feeling does not scale.

---

## What HookScore Does

HookScore breaks a hook down into measurable components and scores each one:

- **Structure** — Is the hook built in a way that naturally leads the viewer forward?
- **Clarity** — Does the viewer immediately understand what this is about?
- **Emotional Trigger** — Does it create curiosity, urgency, fear, or desire?
- **Pattern Interrupt** — Does it break the scroll habit and demand attention?
- **Value Promise** — Does it clearly signal what the viewer will get?

Each component is weighted and combined into a single performance score out of 100.

---

## Why It Exists

Asking "does this sound good?" is the wrong question.

The right question is: **"Will this stop the scroll?"**

HookScore is built to answer that — before you publish, not after you check analytics.

---

## Who It Is For

- Content agencies managing hooks at scale across multiple clients
